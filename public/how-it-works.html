Good to know

Built to remove the guesswork

These are the details that make a trade go smoothly the first time.

Rates locked on confirmation

The rate on your trade is reserved once our team confirms your submission. Volume tiers give better rates on larger trades.

Every trade has a reference

Each submission gets a trade ID like AZ-260101-AB12CD. Use it to track status or reach support.

Card codes stay encrypted

Gift card codes are encrypted at rest and only decrypted by the officer handling your trade. Card images are never shared.

Payouts, not promises

Most verified trades are paid within minutes. If a card needs a second look, you are told why in the trade timeline.

Support on WhatsApp

A real support officer is available during working hours. Send your trade reference and they can check it immediately.

You approve before payout

Check the summary before you submit — amount, rate, payout account. Your confirmation is what authorises the trade.

Questions people ask

Quick answers

How long does a trade take?
Crypto transfers are usually verified in minutes once the network confirms. Gift card checks can take longer on high-value cards or during peak hours. Your timeline shows the status at each stage.
What affects my rate?
Today's market price, the card type, the card's region, and trade size. Larger volumes unlock higher tiers, and rates are reviewed daily. The rate on your submitted trade is shown in your summary.
Can I cancel after submitting?
You can cancel a trade while it is pending or confirmed and before payout is sent. Crypto sent to our wallet is returned to your refund address, minus network fees. Cancellation is not possible after a trade is marked paid.
What if my card is rejected?
If a card cannot be redeemed, the trade is rejected and the exact reason is shown in your dashboard. Common causes are wrong region, already-used codes, or a card with an unexpected balance.
