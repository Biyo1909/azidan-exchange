FAQ

Answers before you trade

How long do payouts take?
Most confirmed trades are paid within 5–15 minutes during support hours. Large or unusual trades may take longer while we complete verification. You'll see the status change on your trade page.
Which gift cards and currencies do you buy?
We buy Apple, Amazon, Steam, Google Play, iTunes, Vanilla, Razer Gold, Sephora, Nordstrom, Walmart, American Express and more — in USD, GBP, EUR, CAD and AUD. The live rates page shows exactly what's available today.
Do I need an account to see rates?
No. Rates and the calculator are public. You only need an account to submit a trade, so we can track it and pay you securely.
How are your rates decided?
Our desk sets rates from what we can settle each asset for on a given day, then publishes a single buying rate for everyone. Rates are reviewed daily — check the "updated" time on the rate panel.
Is it safe to submit my gift card code?
Your code is encrypted before it is stored and is only decrypted by the reviewer handling your trade. We also log every time a code is viewed. Never send card details to anyone outside this platform or our verified WhatsApp number.
Which payout methods do you support?
Bank transfer, OPay, PalmPay, Kuda and Moniepoint. Add your account details when you start the trade — you can save them for next time.
