Trading

Rates and payouts

How do I know today's rate?
The live rates page shows the buying rate for every crypto and gift card we accept. Rates refresh automatically while you browse, and the calculator converts any amount to Naira instantly.
Is the rate fixed when I submit?
Your submitted rate is captured with the trade. Once our team confirms your asset, that rate is locked for that trade. If a card or transfer fails verification, we will contact you before changing anything.
Why do bigger trades get better rates?
Higher volume trades qualify for better tiers automatically. The calculator shows the next tier and how much more you need to qualify, and your trade summary always shows the rate actually applied.
How fast is the payout?
Most verified trades are paid within minutes. Payout speed also depends on your bank's processing time. Every status change appears in the trade timeline and as a notification.
Which payout methods do you use?
Bank transfer is standard, and popular wallets such as OPay, PalmPay, Kuda and Moniepoint are supported when enabled. The options available to you are listed when you create a trade.
Gift cards

Card checks

What details do you need for a gift card?
The card type, the region, the face value, the card code (or e-code) and a clear photo of the physical card or receipt. Extra notes help our officer verify the card faster.
Is my card code safe?
Card codes are encrypted before they are stored. Only the officer processing your trade can decrypt yours, and that access is logged. Nobody at AZIDAN EXCHANGE will ask you for your card code on social media.
Why can a card be rejected?
Codes that have already been redeemed, cards from an unsupported region, or cards whose balance differs from what was submitted are the usual causes. The rejection reason is always shown on the trade page.
Can I sell a card I already scanned?
Never share a card code with more than one buyer. If a code has already been used elsewhere, the trade will fail. Only send codes through this website, never through direct messages.
Account & safety

Your account

Do I need an account to see rates?
No. Rates, the calculator, and all public pages are open to everyone. You need an account to submit a trade and track its payout.
How do I track a trade?
Every trade has a reference like AZ-260101-AB12CD and a page showing its timeline. Open it from your dashboard, or send the reference to support on WhatsApp.
What should I do if I suspect fraud?
Stop the conversation and contact us on the official WhatsApp number shown on this site. We will never ask for your password, your card code by chat, or a payment to release a trade.
How do I reach support?
Use the WhatsApp button on any page or the contact page. Include your trade reference so we can pull up the details immediately.
